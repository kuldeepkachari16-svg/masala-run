# Masala Run — Playtest Round 1

#project/masala-run

**Goal:** find out if the core loop *teaches itself* and *pulls a retry* — without us explaining anything. 3–5 friends, ~10 min each.

## Rules for us (the observers)
- **Say nothing.** No "you attack with what you ate", no "there's a boss". If they ask how to play, say "figure it out, that's the test."
- Watch silently. Note *where they get confused*, not just what they say.
- Let them quit on their own. Don't nudge a retry.

## The 3 questions that matter (ask AFTER they stop)
1. **Did you figure out that you attack with whatever you last ate — on your own?** (If yes: how long did it take? If no: what did you think was happening?)
2. **Did you discover a recipe / the powers without being told?** (Which one? Did you understand *why* it happened?)
3. **When did you stop playing, and why?** (Bored? Too hard? Phone died? Won? "One more go"?)

## Things to watch for silently (don't ask — observe)
- Do they move *toward* food or ignore it?
- Do they panic when flavor fades to PLAIN, or not notice?
- Do they ever use the right-side power buttons? (or never touch them)
- The boss: relief, or frustration?
- First death: do they immediately tap REPLAY, or put the phone down?

## The one decision this feeds — Gate 1
- **Most testers retried unprompted** → loop works → build Phase 2 (act 2, more flavors).
- **They didn't** → the loop is the problem. Fix it before building anything else.

Bring tuning notes too: if everyone says "too hard at wave X", that's a `CONFIG` tweak (see [TUNING.md](TUNING.md)) — live-adjustable on their phone mid-session via the console.
