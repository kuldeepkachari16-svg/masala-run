# Visual Decision Log

## Purpose

This document is the permanent historical record of frozen creative decisions made
during development. It is not the Art Bible. It records decisions that explain why
the Art Bible changed, what was decided, and which chapters are affected.

## Entry Format

Each entry should include:

- Decision ID
- Topic
- Decision
- Rationale
- Status
- Date
- Related Art Bible Chapter(s)
